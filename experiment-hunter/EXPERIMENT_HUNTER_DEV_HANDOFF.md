## Summary

### Title
Always-Visible Add to Cart on Product Cards — Homepage

### Hypothesis
If the Add to Cart button is always visible on product cards instead of appearing only on hover, then homepage add-to-cart click rate and cart creation rate will increase, because removing the hover-discovery barrier lets users act on purchase intent without an extra interaction step.
