## Summary

### Title
Persistent Add to Cart Button on Best Seller Cards

### Hypothesis
If Add to Cart buttons are always visible on Best Seller product cards, then homepage add-to-cart rate will increase significantly, because visible affordances reduce interaction cost and signal purchasability without requiring hover discovery.
