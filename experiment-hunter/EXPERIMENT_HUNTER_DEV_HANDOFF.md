## Summary

### Title
Make product card add-to-cart buttons always visible

### Hypothesis
If we change desktop product cards so add-to-cart buttons are always visible instead of appearing only on hover, then homepage add-to-cart click rate will improve, because the purchase action becomes visible without discovery effort and is less likely to be missed by keyboard or non-hover users.

### Arms
- **Control:** Product cards display image, name, reviews, and price, while the add-to-cart button appears only on hover.
- **Treatment:** Product cards always show a full-width add-to-cart button beneath the price, with the hover state only changing color.

### Metrics
Homepage add-to-cart click rate; Product card click-through rate; Revenue per homepage visitor; Cart starts from homepage
